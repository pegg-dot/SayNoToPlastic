# Say No to Plastic wordmark candidate — review gate

This is a **candidate system, not final brand approval**. It exists so the website can be reviewed against Dr. Haddad's transcript request for a memorable, professional—not groovy—signature identity that can extend to the header, email, social, print, and future merchandise.

## Transcript details preserved

- “Say No to Plastic” should become the movement identity rather than “Homo Plasticus.”
- The mark should feel professional, distinctive, and reusable across logos and merchandise.
- Dr. Haddad explicitly rejected a “groovy” direction.
- He suggested a gold treatment for Say No to Plastic while surrounding text could remain white.
- Titles and conversion labels should not feel undersized.

## Candidate files

- `public/brand/sntp-wordmark-horizontal.svg`
- `public/brand/sntp-wordmark-compact.svg`
- `public/brand/sntp-wordmark-monochrome.svg`

The live header uses selectable HTML text with the same gold wordmark hierarchy with surrounding navigation/copy remaining ivory/white rather than relying on an image for essential navigation.

## Typeface boundary

The project exposes a display stack compatible with the blueprint's Cormorant Garamond / DM Serif direction and a sans stack compatible with Inter. **No final typeface license or bundled font file is claimed.** The final signature face/lettering must be approved and licensed before the mark is treated as final or sent to merchandise/print vendors.

## Clear space

For the horizontal mark, preserve clear space equal to at least the cap height of “SAY NO” on all sides. Do not place the mark over detailed anatomy or high-contrast imagery without a solid/quiet backing field.

## Acceptance gate

Review at header size, email-header size, 1200×630 social-share scale, black/white print, and a merchandise mockup. Final approval must confirm: professional, memorable, legible, not generic, not groovy, and consistent with navy/ivory/restrained-gold brand use.
