# v40.16 completion pass

**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Base:** `homo_plasticus_v40_15`  
**Candidate:** `homo_plasticus_v40_16`

## Why this pass exists

v40.15 completed the canonical integration of ten Dr. Haddad-supplied documents. v40.16 closes the tangible experience and operations gaps identified in the post-integration review without redesigning the existing site.

## Completed experience work

### Anatomy depth without another long scroll

The existing six-scene anatomy journey is preserved. A visitor may optionally open four additional reference points—digestive, kidneys, endocrine, and skin—and inspect a nonmodal drawer containing the supplied summary, two supported points, two uncertainty points, source-review status, and the full article link. Escape closes the drawer and focus returns to the initiating hotspot.

### Seven body-system visuals

Every canonical body-system page now has a bespoke conceptual diagram suited to its topic:

- artery/plaque circulation;
- ovary, follicle, uterus, and hormone pathway;
- distributed endocrine gland network;
- paired kidney filtration pathway;
- layered skin barrier;
- digestive passage and barrier;
- maternal–placental–fetal pathway.

These are explanatory interface graphics, not patient-specific reconstructions or proof of mechanism.

### Practical action tools

- The Quick Action Card links to the full exposure-reduction guide.
- Home includes the explicit “Progress, not perfection” transition.
- The exposure guide includes a device-local worksheet with selectable actions, one-month priority, notes, reset, print, and a blank two-page PDF.
- The worksheet repeats the medical, hygiene, food-safety, accessibility, and emergency boundary.

### Community learning and challenges

- The ten-reading curriculum now includes device-local progress.
- Visitors may explicitly opt into a ten-part, 30-day email sequence.
- A durable migration, enrollment state, scheduled outbox jobs, retry/lease handling, restart behavior, unsubscribe cancellation, readiness checks, and operations documentation support eventual activation.
- Lessons are delivered strictly in order. A terminal provider or delivery failure stops the remaining sequence, while a successful provider send advances enrollment before the outbox payload is scrubbed so a final database update can self-heal on retry.
- When an already-active subscriber explicitly joins the series and receives a new preference token, any stale pending welcome job is replaced before delivery.
- A seven-day reset and 30-day practice are available as device-local interactive plans and printable PDFs.

### Media support

Media and Press Kit now expose six downloadable one-page briefing PDFs:

- cardiovascular system;
- pregnancy and early life;
- female reproductive health;
- detection methods;
- plastic particles versus endocrine-disrupting chemicals;
- practical exposure reduction.

Each briefing carries its source-review state and language to avoid.

## Publication boundaries retained

- Source-review body-system pages remain `noindex,follow`.
- No respiratory/lungs article was invented because no dedicated source document was supplied.
- Interactive tools are educational, not detox plans, treatments, diagnoses, or outcome guarantees.
- The learning series remains inactive until provider, secret, scheduler, migration, and test-recipient acceptance are complete.
- Production Sites v30, domain, hosting, storage, commerce mode, and rollback remain untouched.
