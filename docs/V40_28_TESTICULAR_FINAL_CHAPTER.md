# v40.28 Anatomy Sequence

The homepage anatomy journey contains ten steps in this order:

1. Whole body and circulation
2. Brain
3. Heart and arteries
4. Pregnancy and placenta
5. Ovary and developing eggs
6. Endocrine and metabolic
7. Kidneys and urinary system
8. Skin and exterior surface
9. Digestive system
10. Testicular tissue

The ordering is shared by the visible chapter navigation, scroll progress, story panels, camera states, opacity transitions, particle field, and fallback imagery. Testicular tissue intentionally closes the sequence as a distinct male reference. It remains separate from the female complete-body composite to avoid a misleading sex-specific overlay.
