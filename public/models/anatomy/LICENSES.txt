Female body and reproductive reference additions
Assets: body-female.glb, vasculature-female.glb, uterus-female.glb, pelvis-female.glb

Purpose: the website presents the uterus within the female pelvis as an educational orientation before the placental evidence chapter. The pregnancy exterior is a localized deformation of the HRA skin surface used only to orient the viewer; it is not a patient-specific scan.

Medical Vision Group fetal MRI surface
Asset: fetus-mri.glb
Source: https://github.com/MedicalVisionGroup/fetal-smpl
Paper: Fetuses Made Simple: Modeling and Tracking of Fetal Shape and Pose, MICCAI 2025
Public demonstration subject: MAP-C507, segmentation frame 50
License: MIT License, as published with the source repository
Modifications: source vertices and triangle topology are unchanged; the export recenters the surface and computes vertex normals. The website changes material, scale, rotation, and position at runtime to align the fetal surface with the HRA amnion and placenta for an educational assembly.
Scientific boundary: the public demonstration does not state gestational age. The fetal surface and HRA maternal structures are derived from different datasets and are not represented as a single-patient reconstruction. The assembly is for anatomical orientation, not diagnosis or measurement.

Interactive extended-system viewer (v40.17)
Exterior asset: body-female.glb (local, previously attributed above).
Runtime reference surfaces: VH_F_Kidney_L.glb, VH_F_Kidney_R.glb, VH_F_Ureter_L.glb, VH_F_Ureter_R.glb, VH_F_Urinary_Bladder.glb, VH_F_Small_Intestine.glb, SBU_F_Intestine_Large.glb, VH_F_Liver.glb, VH_F_Pancreas.glb, VH_F_Thymus.glb, VH_F_Ovary_L.glb, VH_F_Ovary_R.glb, VH_F_Fallopian_Tube_L.glb, VH_F_Fallopian_Tube_R.glb.
Canonical release directory: https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-releases/tree/main/v1.2/models
Primary runtime delivery: https://cdn.humanatlas.io/hra-releases/v1.2/models/
Fallback delivery: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-releases/main/v1.2/models/
Library overview: https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
Modifications: geometry is not edited by the site. The viewer clones the supplied scenes at runtime and adapts materials, opacity, lighting, camera framing, and layer visibility. The endocrine viewer intentionally labels its limited structure set and does not fabricate thyroid, adrenal, pituitary, or other missing surfaces.
Operational note: external organ surfaces from the HRA lazy-load when a visitor approaches an extended scroll chapter or opens a focused/complete anatomy viewer. The complete-body atlas requests the registered composite model set, while the local exterior shell and the six core local anatomy layers remain available without those remote files. A working internet connection is required for the first load of remote HRA surfaces; later visits may benefit from the browser cache. Readable fallbacks and retry controls remain available when a remote model cannot be reached.
